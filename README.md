# IBM HR Employee Attrition Analysis 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **employee attrition and workforce trends** using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into factors that may influence employee turnover, including department, job role, age, job satisfaction, monthly income, overtime, business travel, and marital status.

The goal is to help HR teams understand employee attrition patterns and identify areas where employee retention can be improved.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculations and KPIs
* **Excel/CSV** – Dataset and data preparation

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Employee Attrition Overview
* Department-wise Attrition Analysis
* Job Role-wise Attrition
* Age Group Analysis
* Gender-wise Attrition
* Job Satisfaction Analysis
* Monthly Income Analysis
* Overtime Analysis
* Business Travel Analysis
* Marital Status Analysis
* Attrition Trend Analysis
* Interactive Filters & Slicers

## 🔑 Key Insights

👥 **Employee Attrition**

* Younger employees show relatively higher attrition.
* Some departments and job roles have higher turnover.

😊 **Job Satisfaction**

* Lower job satisfaction is associated with higher attrition.
* Overtime may negatively affect employee retention.

💰 **Compensation & Travel**

* Employees with comparatively lower income show higher attrition.
* Frequent business travel may contribute to higher turnover.

🎯 **HR Recommendation**

* Focus on career growth, fair compensation, employee satisfaction, and work-life balance to improve retention.
